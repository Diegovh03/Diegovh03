## 🎓 Current Research — Chalmers University of Technology

### Dynamic modelling of battery systems

My PhD research focuses on developing an integrated **dMFA–LCA framework** for analysing battery systems under changing technological and circular-economy scenarios.

The modelling framework combines yearly information on:

**Battery demand → stocks → inflows → lifetimes → outflows → reuse → recycling → secondary materials → environmental impacts**

A key objective is to connect physical changes in battery systems directly with Life Cycle Inventory and environmental impact calculations.

Current methodological interests include:

* Stock-driven and dynamic MFA modelling
* Product and battery hierarchies
* Lifetime distributions
* Battery reuse and cascading applications
* Recycling and secondary material recovery
* Open-loop and closed-loop recycling modelling
* Prospective environmental assessment
* Dynamic coupling between MFA and LCA
* Scenario and sensitivity analysis
